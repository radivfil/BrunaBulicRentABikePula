Why rent from us

  • Our own workshop

    We are not resellers. Every bike goes through our workshop before and after each rental, and a mechanic is on site during opening hours.

  • Gear included

    Pump, lock, spare tube, patches and tyre levers come with every bike. No small print.

  • We bring them to you

    Apartment, campsite or marina — we deliver and collect the bikes at your address at an agreed time.

  • Route advice

    Maps, trails and honest recommendations for your fitness level. We have ridden here all our lives and know where the shade is in August.
