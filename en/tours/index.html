Rent a Bike Pula/Guided tours

Guided tours

Cape Kamenjak, Istrian gravel and taverns you cannot reach by car. Guide, bike and gear included.

Tours are in preparation — this content is a proposal, not an offer.

  • Easy

    Mountain

    Kamenjak — half day

    Cape Kamenjak and the coves of Premantura

    Gravel tracks through the Kamenjak nature park to the southernmost point of Istria. Flat, firm and wide — rideable by anyone who can sit on a bike. We stop at the Safari bar and at the cove where the water is clearest around noon.

    Distance
    28 km
    Ascent
    260 m
    Duration
    4 h
    per person
    45 €
  • Moderate

    Electric

    Inland and konoba

    Full-day e-bike tour into the hinterland

    On e-bikes from the sea towards inland Istria — through Šišan, Ližnjan and gravel roads towards Barban. There is climbing, but with a motor you will not remember it. The middle of the day is lunch at a family konoba, with home-made pasta and oil from their own olive grove.

    Distance
    52 km
    Ascent
    720 m
    Duration
    7 h
    per person
    85 €
  • Easy

    City

    Pula along the coast

    Easy city tour, three hours

    On city bikes from the Arena along the coast towards Verudela and the Golden Rocks. All flat and paved, without a single serious climb — a tour for families, for the first day of a holiday, or for anyone who simply wants to see the city without a car.

    Distance
    18 km
    Ascent
    90 m
    Duration
    3 h
    per person
    35 €
