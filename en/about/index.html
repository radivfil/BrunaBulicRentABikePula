About us

B.R.B. Sport — bike service, sales and rental in Šišan near Pula.

Cycling is not only a sport or a hobby. It is the best way to see Istria — closer to the landscape and closer to the people than from a car or a boat. But for the full experience you also need a decent bike.

B.R.B. Sport is a family business in Šišan near Pula. We have sold bikes retail and wholesale for years, and we specialise in service — assembly, repair and maintenance. That is why the bikes we rent out go through the same workshop as the ones we sell.

Our bikes suit everyone: people who ride twice a year, people who plan their holiday around routes, and people who arrive with their own pedals. We are happy to help you choose a bike, and to advise on trails, maps and anything else that makes a holiday in Istria better.

Drop by, call or write — we are here.
