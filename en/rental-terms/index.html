Rent a Bike Pula/Rental terms

Rental terms

Our bikes are rented out in technically perfect and clean condition. Please satisfy yourself of this at handover and report any defects immediately.

  1. 01Handling the bike

    The client undertakes to handle the bike with care and to always secure it with the lock. At night the bike must be parked in a secured, lockable room.

  2. 02Theft

    In case of theft of the bike, the client is liable for the amount of the current sale price according to our price list.

  3. 03What comes with the bike

    Every bike is handed over with a pump, lock, spare tube, puncture repair kit and tyre levers. Loss of the pump or lock is charged at €10.00, as is a used tube. Final cleaning is done by us.

  4. 04Damage

    The client is liable for damage caused by falls or improper handling. Any repairs made during the rental must be paid locally by the client.

  5. 05Third-party workshops

    We assume no costs for repairs carried out in third-party workshops during the rental period. Our own workshop is staffed throughout opening hours.

  6. 06Liability

    The client is responsible for themselves in Croatia. Our pick-up service is charged separately. Liability claims against third parties are the responsibility of the client.

  7. 07Payment

    Our rental prices are fixed and payable on taking over the bike. If the bike is returned early no money is refunded — including for bad weather, illness or other reasons.

  8. 08Identification

    We rent our bikes only on presentation of an identity card or passport.

  9. 09Delivery and return

    Bike delivery and return take place daily from 08:00 to 20:00. If the bike is not returned by the close of business at the end of the rental, we reserve the right to charge a full daily rate.

Cancellation

  • A cancellation must be made by email to info.brbsport@gmail.com.
  • Cancel up to 14 days before your rental — 100 % of your payment is refunded.
  • Cancel up to 7 days before your rental — 50 % of your payment is refunded.
  • Cancellation from the 6th day before arrival — the payment is forfeited.
